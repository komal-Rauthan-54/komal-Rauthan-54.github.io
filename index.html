AbhayaAI — Agentic Women Safety Platform

An agentic AI platform for women's safety built on three layers: Prevention, Real-time Escalation, and Post-incident FIR accountability. Grounded in Indian legal frameworks — CrPC Sec.154, Zero FIR, Lalita Kumari judgment. Integrates RAG for legal context retrieval, Redis pub/sub for real-time alerts, WebRTC for live communication, and SHA-256 for tamper-proof evidence hashing.
